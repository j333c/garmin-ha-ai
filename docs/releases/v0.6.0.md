# Release Notes: Garmin HA AI v0.6.0

**Release Date:** 2026-08-22  
**Tag:** `v0.6.0`

Garmin HA AI v0.6.0 is a major milestone release delivering pre-computed 7-day statistical trend analytics, dual-mode polling schedules, full German/English UI localization, multi-account isolation, and a packaged Lovelace custom card with animated circular gauges and an interactive Q&A chat drawer.

---

## 🌟 Key Highlights

### 1. 📊 7-Day Trend Analytics & Mathematical Baselines (Epic 5)
- Pre-computes 7-day rolling statistical means (Sleep Score, Resting HR, Stress Level, Daily Steps, Calories).
- Real-time arithmetic delta computations against 7-day averages and personalized user goals.
- Multi-day HRV transition analysis and recovery trend categorization (`Stable & Balanced`, `Improving`, `Declining`, `Low`).
- Rich activity data ingestion: Aerobic & Anaerobic Training Effect, Average & Maximum Heart Rate, and Heart Rate Zones.
- Deterministic context injection into Block 2 of the AI coaching prompt, eliminating LLM arithmetic hallucination.

### 2. 🛡️ Centralized Error Taxonomy, i18n & Dual-Mode Polling (Epic 6)
- Strongly typed exception hierarchy (`GarminAIError`, `GarminError`, `GarminRateLimitError`, `AIEngineError`, `AIEngineQuotaError`).
- 100% full localization in English (`en.json`) and German (`de.json`) across all flows, options, entity states, and services.
- Flexible Dual-Mode Polling Cadence:
  - **Daily Fixed Time**: Syncs daily at a chosen time (e.g. 06:00:00).
  - **Periodic Interval**: Syncs recurringly across configurable intervals (every 1, 3, 6, 12, or 24 hours).

### 3. 👥 Multi-Account & Multi-Member Household Isolation (Epic 7)
- Independent storage partitioning for tokens and rolling history per config entry ID (`garmin_ha_ai_tokens_{entry_id}.json`, `garmin_ha_ai_history_{entry_id}.json`).
- Seamless migration of existing legacy single-account storage.
- Dedicated Device scope per user account in Home Assistant Device Registry (`Garmin AI (user@example.com)`).
- Per-account fitness goals, coaching personas, and targeted service execution.

### 4. 🎨 Packaged Custom Lovelace Dashboard Card (`custom:garmin-ha-ai-card`) (Epic 8)
- Native custom Lovelace card featuring animated SVG circular gauges for Sleep Score, Body Battery, and Stress Level.
- Recovery glance badges for HRV status, Resting Heart Rate, and Steps.
- Instant tab switcher between 1-2 sentence executive summary and full markdown coaching report.
- Embedded interactive Q&A chat drawer for direct AI coach conversations.
- Registered with Home Assistant Visual Card Picker (`window.customCards`).
