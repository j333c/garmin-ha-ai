- (decision) Selected product concept garmin-ha-ai: a Home Assistant custom component integrating Garmin Connect data with AI analysis.
- (decision) AI Provider strategy: Primary support for Google Gemini with configurable support for standard LLM APIs (OpenAI format).
- (decision) Garmin ingestion via python-garminconnect library with token-based OAuth and MFA support, storing statistics locally in Home Assistant.
- (decision) Output formats include HA dashboard entities (short/long text), notifications (push/email), and interactive Q&A service call against local health history.
